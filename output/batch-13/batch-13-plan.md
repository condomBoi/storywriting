## M001 | The Matching Scar → Lost Sister Found

**Engine:** E08 — Reunion / Recognition
**Opening style:** Object Clue
**Concept:** A waitress and a regular notice identical star scars from the same childhood fire that separated two foster sisters.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Setup / Clue appears):**
Megan, 28, a waitress at a diner in Columbus, Ohio, notices her Tuesday regular Elena rolling up her sleeve — revealing a star-shaped burn scar on her inner forearm identical to Megan's own.

**Beat 2 — Build (Connection deepens):**
Megan keeps serving Elena week after week, each time catching glimpses — the same nervous habit of covering the scar, the same way she flinches at loud sounds. Megan pulls her old foster file from under her bed: a fire, age 4, a sister separated.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Recognition):**
Elena mentions she was adopted from Ohio foster care after a house fire. Megan's hands shake as she sets down the coffee. She pulls her sleeve up. Elena's face goes white.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Reunion):**
DNA confirms they're sisters. Twenty-four years of searching ends over a $3.50 cup of coffee. Elena had been coming to the diner because it was the last address in her file.

### Character Lock
- **Megan Torres** — 28, dark curly hair in a messy bun, diner uniform (teal polo + black apron), star-shaped burn scar on left inner forearm
- **Elena Byrne** — 30, straight dark hair, olive cardigan over white tee, identical star scar on right inner forearm
- **Setting** — Rosie's Diner, Columbus OH, morning shift, warm fluorescent light + window sun
- **Object clue** — the matching star-shaped burn scars

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Megan mid-pour at Elena's booth, eyes locked on Elena's exposed forearm scar. Elena hasn't noticed Megan staring. The coffee overflows slightly. Natural morning diner light.

## M002 | Tipped a Dollar → $1.2M Bond

**Engine:** E00 — Humiliation → Reveal
**Opening style:** Dialogue Slap
**Concept:** A wealthy family tips the gravedigger a single dollar, then he hands the youngest daughter a sealed envelope — inside is a savings bond her mother hid from the family, worth $1.2 million.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Humiliation):**
At a private cemetery in Greenwich, CT, the Aldridge family — three adult siblings in black Prada — tip the gravedigger Marcus a single crumpled dollar after their mother's burial. "For your trouble," the eldest sneers.

**Beat 2 — Build (Clue):**
Marcus, 58, pockets the dollar calmly. He reaches into his worn jacket and pulls out a sealed manila envelope. He walks past the three siblings and hands it directly to the youngest daughter, Claire, 26, who the family has always dismissed.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Recognition begins):**
Claire opens the envelope: a US Treasury savings bond in her name only, purchased 25 years ago, now matured at $1.2 million. A handwritten note from her mother: "They'll take everything. This is yours. Marcus knows."

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Twist + consequence):**
The siblings' lawyers confirm the bond is untouchable — it's Claire's sole property, outside the estate. Marcus was her mother's groundskeeper for 30 years and the only person she trusted. The family's $1 tip was the last insult their mother ever anticipated.

### Character Lock
- **Marcus Webb** — 58, weathered Black man, gray stubble, faded navy work jacket, dirt under nails, calm dignified posture
- **Claire Aldridge** — 26, youngest sibling, mousy brown hair, simple black dress (no designer labels), red-rimmed eyes
- **Richard Aldridge** — 42, eldest, slicked hair, black Prada overcoat, gold watch
- **Setting** — Evergreen Hills Cemetery, Greenwich CT, overcast late morning, bare trees
- **Object clue** — sealed manila envelope

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Marcus accepting the crumpled dollar bill from Richard's outstretched hand. Claire standing apart from her siblings, head bowed. The open grave behind them. Overcast cemetery light.

## M003 | The Familiar Perfume → Mother Found

**Engine:** E08 — Reunion / Recognition
**Opening style:** Letter Fragment
**Concept:** A nurse catches a scent on a new patient and knows it's the mother who left when she was four.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Scent trigger):**
Danielle, 32, an ER nurse in Nashville, leans in to check vitals on a new elderly patient — and freezes. The perfume. Shalimar. The exact scent from her only memory of being held as a child before her mother vanished.

**Beat 2 — Build (Evidence accumulates):**
The patient's chart says "Patricia Okafor, 56." Danielle's birth certificate says "Mother: Patricia N. Okafor." The patient has a faded C-section scar. Danielle's hands tremble through every routine check. She can't ask. She can't not ask.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Connection):**
Patricia, half-sedated, murmurs: "I had a little girl once… Dani… I dream about her." Danielle drops the chart.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Reunion):**
Patricia left to protect Danielle from an abusive partner — spent 28 years believing Danielle was better off not knowing. Danielle pulls a creased photo from her wallet: herself at 4, on Patricia's lap. Patricia sobs. The Shalimar was the one thing she never changed.

### Character Lock
- **Danielle Okafor-Harris** — 32, medium-brown skin, box braids pinned up, blue nurse scrubs, small gold stud earrings
- **Patricia Okafor** — 56, thin, graying natural hair, hospital gown, faded C-section scar, wears Shalimar perfume
- **Setting** — St. Thomas West ER, Nashville TN, night shift, harsh fluorescent + blue monitor glow
- **Object clue** — Shalimar perfume (scent as object)

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Danielle leaning close to check Patricia's IV, eyes wide, frozen mid-motion. Patricia's eyes closed, unaware. The ER monitors glow blue behind them. Night shift quiet.

## M004 | The Greedy Flipper → Inspector Flips

**Engine:** E05 — Comeuppance
**Opening style:** Accuser POV
**Concept:** A house-flipper lies about a foundation crack to a young couple; the inspector he bribed flips and reports him.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Arrogant act):**
Derek Voss, 45, a house-flipper in Austin TX, slaps fresh drywall over a massive foundation crack and tells the young couple — Jenna and Marco, both 28 — "Structurally perfect. I'd put my own kids here." He winks at his bribed inspector, Gary, who nods.

**Beat 2 — Build (Scheme deepens):**
Derek pressures the couple to close fast — "Three other offers by Friday." He texts Gary: "Keep your mouth shut, another 2K coming." Gary stares at the text. He has a daughter Jenna's age who just bought her first house.

**Beat 3 — Turn (The flip):**
Gary walks into the county licensing board with screenshots, the original inspection photos showing the crack, and a signed statement. He also CC's Jenna and Marco directly.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Self-made downfall):**
Derek loses his contractor license, faces fraud charges, and the county flags all 14 of his recent flips for re-inspection. Jenna and Marco get their deposit back plus damages. Gary loses his bribe money but keeps his license by cooperating.

### Character Lock
- **Derek Voss** — 45, tan, bleached tips, fitted polo, expensive watch, too-white teeth, smug grin
- **Gary Mullins** — 52, overweight, sweat-stained collar, clipboard, conflicted expression
- **Jenna Park** — 28, Korean-American, ponytail, excited first-time-buyer energy, floral blouse
- **Marco Reyes** — 28, Latino, neat beard, button-down, arm around Jenna
- **Setting** — newly flipped craftsman house, Austin TX, midday, bright Texas sun through staged living room
- **Object clue** — fresh drywall patch (hiding crack)

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Derek, all smiles, gesturing at the freshly painted wall while Jenna and Marco look around excitedly. Gary stands behind with clipboard, looking uncomfortable. Bright staged living room.

## M005 | The Garden Bench → Sitting Alone

**Engine:** E06 — Bittersweet / Too Late
**Opening style:** Tender Oddity
**Concept:** A man builds the garden bench his wife always wanted; he sets it the morning of the day he becomes the one who sits there alone.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Quiet devotion):**
Walter, 71, carries hand-sanded cedar planks into the backyard at dawn in suburban Portland, OR. He's been building this bench in secret for three months — his wife Ruth's 50th anniversary gift. Today's the day.

**Beat 2 — Build (Race against time):**
Walter assembles the final pieces, carves "Ruth & Walt — 50 years" into the armrest. He sets two cushions. Brews two cups of tea. Goes inside to wake her. The bedroom is too still.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Too late):**
Ruth passed in her sleep. The tea goes cold. The ambulance comes. The bench sits perfect and empty in morning light.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Grief + love):**
Weeks later, Walter sits on the bench every morning with two cups of tea. Neighbors see one man, two cups. He talks to her. The bench is exactly what she wanted. She never saw it.

### Character Lock
- **Walter Brennan** — 71, white hair, flannel shirt sleeves rolled up, reading glasses on forehead, sawdust on hands
- **Ruth Brennan** — 69 (deceased), referenced but not shown in photo — gray bob, blue cardigan (in Walter's memory)
- **Setting** — backyard garden, suburban Portland OR, early dawn, golden first-light through fence slats
- **Object clue** — hand-carved cedar bench with inscription "Ruth & Walt — 50 years"

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Walter kneeling beside the finished bench at dawn, carving the inscription with a pocket knife. Two tea cups sit on the armrest. The back door to the house is slightly ajar. Soft golden light.

## M006 | The Lost Heirloom → Fired for Honesty

**Engine:** E00 — Humiliation → Reveal
**Opening style:** Silent Witness
**Concept:** A hotel denies finding a guest's lost heirloom ring, then the guest posts about it online — a former housekeeper messages her with an incident report number.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Humiliation/Dismissal):**
At The Belmont Hotel in Charleston, SC, guest Vivian Carter reports her grandmother's emerald ring missing from her room. The front desk manager, Philip, tells her smoothly: "Our staff checked thoroughly. Nothing was found. Perhaps you misplaced it?"

**Beat 2 — Build (Pressure + Clue):**
Vivian posts on social media — the ring has sentimental value, not just monetary. Within hours, a DM arrives from Rosa Delgado, a former Belmont housekeeper: "I found your ring 3 days ago. I turned it in. They fired me the next day for 'insubordination.' Here's my incident report number: BH-4471."

**Beat 3 — Turn (Recognition):**
Vivian calls corporate with the incident report number. The report exists. The ring was logged, then the log was deleted by Philip. Rosa was fired for pressing him to return it.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Twist + consequence):**
Philip is terminated. Rosa is offered her job back with back pay. Vivian gets her ring — found in Philip's office desk. The Belmont issues a public apology. Rosa's "insubordination" was integrity.

### Character Lock
- **Vivian Carter** — 38, auburn hair in low bun, linen blazer, distressed but composed, grandmother's emerald ring (missing)
- **Philip Trent** — 44, hotel manager, slicked dark hair, fitted navy suit, practiced smile
- **Rosa Delgado** — 34, former housekeeper, dark hair in braid, no longer in uniform (fired)
- **Setting** — The Belmont Hotel lobby, Charleston SC, mid-afternoon, polished marble + chandelier light
- **Object clue** — incident report number BH-4471

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Vivian at the front desk, leaning forward with worry. Philip behind the counter, hands spread in a dismissive "nothing we can do" gesture. The marble lobby gleams around them. Another housekeeper (Rosa) is visible in the background, watching.

## M007 | The Same Bracelet → Twins Found

**Engine:** E08 — Reunion / Recognition
**Opening style:** Object Clue
**Concept:** Two strangers comparing keepsakes find sequential hospital ID numbers — twins separated at birth.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Object appears):**
At a flea market in Savannah, GA, two women — Kira, 34, and Amara, 34 — reach for the same vintage jewelry box at a booth. They laugh. Kira's wrist has a faded plastic hospital bracelet tied as a charm on her watch band. Amara freezes — she has the same.

**Beat 2 — Build (Numbers align):**
They sit on a bench comparing bracelets. Kira's reads "Baby Girl A — #7751, Mercy General, 1991." Amara's reads "#7752." Sequential. Same hospital. Same year. Same day. Neither knew she was a twin.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Recognition):**
Amara pulls up her adoption papers on her phone. Same mother's name redacted the same way. They look at each other — same nose, same widow's peak, same way they bite their bottom lip.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Reunion):**
DNA test confirms: identical twins, separated at two days old. Mercy General's records show a closed adoption — the birth mother was 16 and couldn't keep both. She left matching bracelets hoping they'd find each other someday.

### Character Lock
- **Kira Fontaine** — 34, light brown skin, natural curls pulled back, denim jacket, hospital bracelet charm on her watch band (#7751)
- **Amara Wells** — 34, light brown skin, same curls but longer, sundress + cardigan, hospital bracelet charm on a necklace (#7752)
- **Setting** — Forsyth Park flea market, Savannah GA, late morning, dappled shade under live oaks
- **Object clue** — matching faded hospital bracelets with sequential ID numbers

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Kira and Amara sitting on a park bench, both holding their wrists/necklace up to compare the faded bracelets. Their faces show dawning shock. Flea market bustle in background, live oak shade.

## M008 | The Cruel Review Bomber → IP Traced

**Engine:** E05 — Comeuppance
**Opening style:** Dialogue Slap
**Concept:** A rival floods a café with fake one-stars; the platform traces every review to his single office IP.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Arrogant act):**
Todd Brennan, 39, owns a corporate coffee chain location in downtown Portland, OR. The indie café across the street — "Maple & Bean," owned by Lucia, 31 — is stealing his foot traffic. Todd sits in his office, grinning, posting his 15th fake one-star review under a new alias.

**Beat 2 — Build (Damage mounts):**
Maple & Bean drops from 4.8 to 3.1 stars in two weeks. Lucia loses catering contracts. She files a dispute with the review platform, crying at her counter after closing. Todd sends his employees to photograph her empty tables as "proof" his chain is superior.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Self-made ruin):**
The review platform's fraud team traces all 47 fake reviews to a single IP address — Todd's office WiFi. They flag it, notify Lucia, and forward the case to the state attorney general's consumer protection division.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Downfall):**
Todd's franchise license is revoked for "conduct unbecoming." The platform permanently bans his business listing. Lucia's rating is restored to 4.8 with a "verified authentic" badge. Local news covers it — Maple & Bean gets a line out the door.

### Character Lock
- **Todd Brennan** — 39, buzz cut, polo with chain logo, smug grin, multiple phones on his desk
- **Lucia Herrera** — 31, Latina, flour-dusted apron, dark hair in messy bun, warm but exhausted eyes
- **Setting** — Todd's office above his chain café, downtown Portland OR, evening, blue laptop glow + overhead fluorescent
- **Object clue** — laptop screen showing review posting interface with multiple tabs/aliases

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Todd at his desk, smirking at his laptop screen (showing review site), multiple phones beside him. Through his office window behind him, you can see Maple & Bean's storefront across the street with its lights on. Evening blue glow.

## M009 | The Returned Ring → Too Late to Propose

**Engine:** E06 — Bittersweet / Too Late
**Opening style:** Letter Fragment
**Concept:** A man finally affords the ring he couldn't years ago and flies to propose again; her sister meets him at the gate with the news.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Devotion/Hope):**
James, 42, a construction foreman in Detroit, walks out of a jewelry store holding a small velvet box. Inside: the exact ring he couldn't afford 15 years ago when Catherine said "not yet, but someday." He books a flight to Tucson.

**Beat 2 — Build (Journey + Anticipation):**
On the plane, James rehearses what he'll say. His phone has a thread of texts with Catherine going back months — "I'll visit soon." "Can't wait." He hasn't told her why. The ring cost him two years of overtime. He lands with shaking hands.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Too late):**
At the arrivals gate, Catherine's sister Linda stands alone. Her eyes are red. She's holding a folded note. "James… she wanted you to have this. She wrote it last week." Catherine passed three days ago. Pancreatic cancer. She never told him.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Grief + love):**
The note reads: "If you're reading this, I'm sorry I didn't tell you. I didn't want the last time you saw me to be about goodbye. Come anyway. Sit in my garden. That's where I'll be." James sits in her garden with the ring box open on his knee. He never got to ask again.

### Character Lock
- **James Whitfield** — 42, broad-shouldered, calloused hands, navy peacoat over flannel, velvet ring box in coat pocket
- **Linda Castillo** — 45, Catherine's older sister, red-rimmed eyes, holding a folded handwritten note
- **Catherine** — (deceased, 40) — referenced in note and texts only
- **Setting** — Tucson International Airport arrivals gate, afternoon, harsh desert sun through floor-to-ceiling windows
- **Object clue** — small velvet ring box + Catherine's handwritten note

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
James walking through the airport arrivals corridor, ring box barely visible in his coat pocket, face full of nervous hope. The Tucson sun blazes through the terminal windows. He's scanning the crowd ahead. No conflict — pure anticipation before heartbreak.

## M010 | The Employee Walkout → Across the Street

**Engine:** E00 — Humiliation → Reveal
**Opening style:** Accuser POV
**Concept:** A toxic owner watches all staff walk out on the busiest day of the year, then sees them across the street helping the competitor who always treated them with respect.

### 4-Beat Arc

**Beat 1 — Hook (Humiliation/Arrogance):**
Craig Hollister, 51, owner of Hollister's Grill in Memphis, TN, screams at his line cook during the lunch rush on the first day of Memphis in May BBQ Fest — the biggest revenue day of the year. "You're all replaceable! Every single one of you!"

**Beat 2 — Build (Walkout):**
One by one, every employee — six cooks, three servers, the dishwasher — removes their apron and walks out mid-service. Craig is left alone with a dining room of 40 orders. He scrambles. Food burns. Customers leave.

**Beat 3 — Turn (Recognition):**
Craig looks out his front window. Across the street, his entire former staff is working the line at "Ray's Smokehouse" — the competitor whose owner, Ray Patterson, attends every one of his employees' kids' graduations. They're laughing. Ray's is packed.

**Beat 4 — Payoff (Consequence):**
Craig's Grill closes permanently three weeks later. Ray's gets a feature in the local paper: "The restaurant that took in ten workers in ten minutes." Craig's Yelp fills with former employee reviews telling the real story.

### Character Lock
- **Craig Hollister** — 51, red-faced, thick neck, grease-stained chef coat, pointing finger, vein in temple
- **Ray Patterson** — 62, Black man, calm smile, clean apron, gray beard, reading glasses tucked in chest pocket
- **The staff** — 10 workers led by head cook Maria, 35, Latina, box braids, visible relief on her face
- **Setting** — Hollister's Grill interior + view across street to Ray's Smokehouse, Memphis TN, midday, hot summer sun
- **Object clue** — pile of removed aprons on the kitchen counter

### Peak-tension moment for photo (Beat 1–2)
Craig screaming at Maria in the kitchen, finger pointed in her face, other staff frozen mid-work watching. Aprons still on but hands clenched. The kitchen is chaotic — orders piling up. Midday harsh kitchen light.
